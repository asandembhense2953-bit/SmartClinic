════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
AS-IS PROCESS ANALYSIS
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Bottleneck identified from the As-Is process model:

  BOTTLENECK: Manual paper-based patient check-in
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Current (As-Is):
    Patient arrives → receptionist searches paper register →
    writes name on whiteboard → calls number manually →
    no visibility of waiting time for patient or staff.

  Problems:
    - Receptionist spends 3–5 min per patient on admin
    - No real-time queue visibility for doctors/nurses
    - No wait-time estimates — patients have no information
    - Occupancy not tracked — clinic can become overcrowded
      without staff being aware

  TO-BE (Automated in Sprint 2):
    Patient scans QR card at kiosk → system auto-assigns
    queue number → calculates wait estimate → all dashboards
    update in real time → occupancy monitored by Sensor 2
    every 10 seconds → warning fires at capacity threshold.

IOT SENSOR DESIGN
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Sensor 1 — SmartCheckIn
  Trigger:   Patient check-in event (QR scan or manual)
  Data:      sensor_type='SmartCheckIn', source, patient_id,
             occupancy_level (current active count)
  Storage:   sensor_readings table
  Purpose:   Logs every check-in event for audit and
             analytics (IS3 requirement)

Sensor 2 — ActiveQueueOccupancy
  Trigger:   setInterval every 10 000 ms (Node.js backend)
  Data:      sensor_type='ActiveQueueOccupancy',
             source='setInterval_Auto',
             occupancy_level (live count of CheckedIn patients)
  Storage:   sensor_readings table
  Purpose:   Continuous occupancy monitoring — fires warning
             at 8+ patients, powers the live occupancy bar
             on the receptionist dashboard
